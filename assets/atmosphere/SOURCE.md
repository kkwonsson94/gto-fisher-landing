# Background reference assets

Downloaded 2026-10-12 for the local Fisher pricing design preview.

- `rays.mp4`: https://n8n.io/videos/rays.mp4 — H.264, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 13.2 seconds, 1,802,330 bytes, no audio track.
- `rays-poster.jpg`: https://n8n.io/images/rays-poster.jpg — original static fallback.
- `grain.png`: https://n8n.io/images/bg-pattern.png — original 2 × 2 repeating texture.
- `pricing-halo.png`: https://n8n.io/images/pricing_page_bg.png — original elliptical silhouette, used as an alpha mask for the second Fisher-colored light field.

These are source-site assets, not AI-generated content. CSS recolors/composites the rays and adds Fisher blue-violet and ice-blue blurred fields plus dark reading masks. No claim of asset ownership or licensing transfer is made.

`included-review.webp`, `included-binoculars.webp`, `included-solver.webp` and `included-coach.webp` are existing transparent Fisher artwork copied unchanged from `assets/landing/gto-landing-assets/01-delivery/images/`. They replace opaque-background illustrations in this preview only.
